Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a pick?
Open your pool and tap "Make Pick" to select a team for the current gameweek.

What's the difference between Survivor and League mode?
Survivor mode gives everyone a set number of lives - lose one on a loss or a missed pick, and you're out once they're gone. League mode has no elimination; everyone plays every week and is ranked by total points instead.

What happens if my team loses (Survivor mode)?
You lose one life. When all your lives are gone, you're eliminated from that pool.

Can I change my pick?
Yes, any time before the deadline for that gameweek (kickoff of the earliest match).

How do I invite friends to my pool?
Share your pool's session code with them - they enter it under "Join Pool" to join instantly.

How do I delete my account or request my data be removed?
Email us at the address above and we'll take care of it.
